# DreamTown Urban Regulation (fictional)

> Synthetic sample text written for the ARCHAI demo. DreamTown does not exist and this
> document has no legal force. Any resemblance to a real regulation is coincidental.

## Article C1 - Zones and definitions

1.1 The town is divided into three zones: R1 (low-density residential), R2 (mid-rise residential) and M1 (mixed use).

1.2 "Building height" means the vertical distance from the lowest ground level to the highest point of the roof, measured in metres.

1.3 "Floor" means a habitable level above ground; plant rooms and roof terraces do not count as floors.

## Article C2 - Building height

2.1 In zone R2, building height shall not exceed 21 m.

2.2 In zone R2, the number of floors shall not exceed 6 floors.

## Article C3 - Density and coverage

3.1 The floor area ratio shall not exceed 2.0.

3.2 Site coverage shall not exceed 45 % of the plot area.

## Article C4 - Setbacks

4.1 The front setback shall be at least 5 m from the plot boundary.

4.2 The side setback shall be at least 3 m from the plot boundary.

## Article C5 - Minimum plot size

5.1 The plot area shall be at least 600 m2 for any new building.

## Article C6 - Parking

6.1 Parking shall be provided at a rate of at least 1 space per 100 m2 of gross floor area.

## Article C7 - Active frontage

7.1 On primary streets, ground-floor active frontage shall be at least 40 % of the building frontage length.

## Article C8 - Design quality

8.1 Buildings shall respect the character of the surrounding neighbourhood.
